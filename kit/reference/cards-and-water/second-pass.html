What changed from the first pass

  • The plates are no longer painted with gradients. Each one is lit live, pixel by pixel, like real metal.
  • Letters are cut into the plate or inlaid, so the light catches their edges.
  • The reflection slides across each plate as you scroll, the way it does on a real plaque.
  • Only brass and bronze now, in four variations.
  • The stream is a ribbon of flowing light, not a line. It pools beside each card and lights the metal as it passes.
